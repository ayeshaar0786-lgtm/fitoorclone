About Fitoor

Fitoor is Pakistan's art store, bringing original hand-painted artworks from local artists into homes and offices.

Every painting is unique. Each piece is shown with its size, medium and details so you can choose with confidence.

Our aim is to make original art easy to find, and to support the artists who create it.
